# Requirement Ledger

Canonical requirement records live here.

Each important requirement record must contain:

`REQ_ID, SPEC_HASH, SPEC_PAGE, SPEC_LINES, SPEC_TEXT, AUTHORITY_CLASS, EXPECTED_BEHAVIOR, FORBIDDEN_BEHAVIOR, AFFECTED_SYSTEMS, DEPENDENCIES, IMPLEMENTATION_PATHS, TEST_PATHS, STATUS, LAST_VERIFIED_SHA`.

No requirement may be promoted to VERIFIED without traceable source, executed evidence, review, integration, and fresh verification on the relevant SHA.
