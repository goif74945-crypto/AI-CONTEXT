# AI-Proposed Future Extensions

Everything here is **proposal only**.

1. **View Contract Replay** — persist input/output fingerprint pairs plus authoritative references to reconstruct why a historical surface looked as it did without hidden reasoning.
2. **Accessibility Truth Floor** — make critical banners/confirmations/blocked state mandatory across keyboard, screen reader, low-vision, and reduced-motion modes.
3. **Device Surface Profiles** — compile identical mandatory truth into mobile/desktop/CLI layout constraints.
4. **Regret-Minimizing Confirmation** — friction proportional to irreversibility/recoverability.
5. **Explicit Operator Expertise Profiles** — user-selected detail preference, never silently inferred.
6. **Surface Contract Snapshots** — detect UI refactors that mask FREEZE or change disabled-state meaning.
7. **Versioned Risk-to-Disclosure Schema** — only after explicit spec promotion.
8. **Cross-Renderer Conformance** — prove web/mobile/CLI preserve mandatory semantics from one SurfacePlan.
9. **Privacy-Safe Cognitive Budget Telemetry** — aggregate expansion/cancellation friction only, no emotional profiling.
10. **Formal Policy Model** — future reachability proof after policy is actually promoted.

None of these items is current NEXY build scope merely because it appears in this supplemental package.
