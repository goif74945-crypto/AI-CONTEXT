# NEXY::PRISM UX Laws

## Goal
Make NEXY feel simpler without making truth simpler than it actually is.

- **U1 Minimum safe surface:** show the least optional complexity required to understand state, legal action, and consequence.
- **U2 Mandatory truth:** FREEZE, STOP, unverified output, critical risk, irreversible action, blocked action, and conflict cannot be removed by compact mode.
- **U3 Preference raises transparency:** user detail preference may expose more but never lower the truth floor.
- **U4 Disabled means why:** disabled primary action carries a deterministic reason code.
- **U5 No optimistic success fiction:** loading/running/consensus/unverified never compile to verified-final.
- **U6 Irreversibility must feel irreversible:** destructive actions do not share ordinary navigation confirmation.
- **U7 Authority appears when relevant:** permission boundaries are explicit when they block action without cluttering every normal screen.
- **U8 Diagnostic depth follows severity:** failure/freeze/stop/conflict/critical risk automatically expose more structure.

Expected benefits are design hypotheses until validated by user research and E2E product testing.
