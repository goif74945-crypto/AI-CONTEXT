# Research Backlog — Before Any Promotion

All items are AI-PROPOSED research questions, not current NEXY obligations.

1. **Metadata provenance protocol:** sign provider capability/clearance/price records and define TTL/freshness.
2. **Token-estimation uncertainty:** derive conservative envelopes for tool-heavy and long-context tasks.
3. **Budget reservation:** prevent concurrent plans from oversubscribing a shared operator budget.
4. **Pareto frontier proof:** optimize large inventories without changing exhaustive deterministic selection semantics.
5. **Provider independence ontology:** distinguish provider, model family, infrastructure region, ownership and shared failure domains.
6. **Verifier diversity policy:** test whether independence should be stronger than provider-domain inequality for critical tasks.
7. **Failure-rate feedback:** allow historical reliability to influence optimization without becoming an authority substitute.
8. **Privacy locality:** represent on-device, private-VPC, regional and external-provider execution constraints.
9. **Carbon/energy budgets:** optional operator-controlled metric that cannot outrank hard evidence/security gates.
10. **Burst vs sustained latency:** model queueing, rate-limit and cold-start distributions deterministically enough for planning.
11. **Capability attestation:** replace self-declared capability strings with test-backed signed capability records.
12. **Resource-envelope versioning:** guarantee plan replay refers to exact inventory/policy/pricing revisions.
13. **Multi-stage task graphs:** allocate resources across dependent subtasks without local minima breaking global budget feasibility.
14. **Fallback reservations:** reserve enough budget for failover without double-spending the primary allocation.
15. **Adversarial pricing:** test zero/negative/overflow/rapidly changing price feeds and integer-bound safety.
16. **Quality metric governance:** determine whether quality score belongs in canonical routing at all, and if so, how it is evidenced.
17. **Unknown metadata:** define which missing fields are hard freeze vs safely ineligible candidate.
18. **Real-world benchmark harness:** replay anonymized task profiles against provider snapshots without touching production execution.
19. **Audit compression:** retain explainability without O(W×V) log explosion.
20. **Formal invariant suite:** property-test that no selected plan violates any gate under randomized inventories and budgets.
