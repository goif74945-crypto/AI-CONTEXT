# Research & Divergence Record

## Project-grounded facts

AI-CONTEXT requires least authority, data minimization when interacting with external services, source/evidence separation, and explicit freeze behavior under unresolved material ambiguity. NEXY context describes external AI models as workers rather than authority and preserves a trusted control boundary around law, verification, and output release.

The current normalized NEXY source denominator is 837 requirement rows. This lab neither changes that denominator nor asserts that NPCEF is included in current build obligations.

## Divergence check

A scan of the 100 most recent AI-CONTEXT commits before this lab found substantial sibling work in proof-carrying execution, context compilation, scope control, failure/counterfactual verification, knowledge decay, temporal truth, evidence graphs, uncertainty/proof debt, capability admission, capacity economics, compatibility/migration/reversibility, reliability budgets, epistemic governance, user experience compilation, human-authority integrity, and clarification optimization.

No dedicated recent sibling project was identified whose primary contract is **purpose-bound, recipient-bound, field-minimized context egress with value-free receipts**. Absence is limited to the inspected recent commit scope; this is not a universal claim over all historical artifacts.

## External rationale, not NEXY authority

Primary/current privacy guidance supports the research direction:
- purpose limitation: data collected for one explicit purpose should not silently flow into unrelated purposes;
- minimization: process/disclose only data relevant and necessary to the declared purpose;
- lifecycle privacy-risk management: privacy cannot be bolted on only at storage time;
- LLM applications face sensitive-information disclosure and prompt-leakage risks at model/tool boundaries.

NPCEF converts those broad concerns into an engineering experiment. It does **not** claim GDPR/NIST/OWASP compliance. Legal compliance depends on jurisdiction, controller/processor roles, lawful basis, rights handling, retention, notices, contracts, technical/organizational controls, and real operational evidence outside this prototype.

## Research hypothesis

A deterministic egress compiler with explicit purpose/recipient/expiry/consent metadata can reduce unnecessary context exposure while producing more auditable and testable release decisions than ad-hoc prompt construction.

## Falsification conditions

The proposal should be rejected or redesigned if experiments show that it:
- routinely blocks legitimate workflows because metadata cannot be maintained accurately;
- permits meaningful leakage through nested, transformed, streamed, or derived data paths;
- creates consent fatigue that users cannot reason about;
- produces receipts that themselves leak sensitive identifiers;
- cannot maintain deterministic decisions across distributed policy/version boundaries;
- cannot enforce revocation before downstream use;
- materially increases attack surface or latency without proportional privacy benefit.
