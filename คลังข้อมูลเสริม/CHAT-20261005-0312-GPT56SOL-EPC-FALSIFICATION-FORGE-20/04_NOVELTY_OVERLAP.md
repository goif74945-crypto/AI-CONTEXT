## Known overlaps that are intentional

- Determinism appears throughout NEXY. DPG does not implement NEXY determinism; it creates an experimental replay obligation.
- Resource/latency concepts appear in integration labs. REP does not calculate adoption compatibility; it generates boundary experiments.
- Evidence handling appears in many labs. DES/EIYE/OTP choose evidence acquisition experiments; they do not validate canonical evidence schemas or JUDGE decisions.
- Reproducibility exists elsewhere as a concern. RCB is a local canonical experiment capsule and explicitly uses a non-security checksum; SHA-256 artifact identity remains external evidence.

## Novelty conclusion

FACT: no inspected prior EPC mission listed this complete proposal-to-falsification-experiment pipeline or the locked twenty mechanisms above.

INFERENCE: the system is semantically orthogonal enough to retain as a distinct Lo4 candidate because its primary responsibility is **experimental falsification design**, while neighboring systems primarily own court governance, integration ecology, adversarial integration validation or combinatorial scenario generation.

UNKNOWN: unindexed/unread files elsewhere in the repository could contain conceptually similar academic or experimental-design logic under unrelated terminology. This audit therefore supports KEEP consideration but is not a universal proof of originality.
