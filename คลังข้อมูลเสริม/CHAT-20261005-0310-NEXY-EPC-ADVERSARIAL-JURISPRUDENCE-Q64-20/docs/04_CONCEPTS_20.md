# Twenty Lo4 Adversarial Jurisprudence Systems

All systems below are **AI-proposed Lo4 concepts**. None are Canon until formal promotion by the existing authority chain.

| # | ID | System | Executable function | Core invariant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SIC-01 | Source Independence Chamber | `sourceIndependenceChamber` | Correlated evidence cannot masquerade as independent corroboration. |
| 2 | CTG-02 | Collusion Topology Guard | `collusionTopologyGuard` | Shared affiliations/candidate links beyond policy block adjudication. |
| 3 | MPS-03 | Minority Proof Shield | `minorityProofShield` | Strong DIRECT/FORMAL/TEST minority evidence forces review rather than majority suppression. |
| 4 | CSG-04 | Counterargument Sufficiency Gate | `counterargumentSufficiencyGate` | An affirmative verdict must represent the opposing case. |
| 5 | SPEF-05 | Single-Point Evidence Fragility | `singlePointEvidenceFragility` | Verdict support must survive removal of any single evidence point. |
| 6 | CVS-06 | Counterfactual Verdict Stability Chamber | `counterfactualVerdictStability` | A required fraction of leave-one-out worlds must still satisfy the burden. |
| 7 | QDG-07 | Quorum Diversity Gate | `quorumDiversityGate` | Minimum actor/family diversity is non-compensatory. |
| 8 | COIF-08 | Conflict-of-Interest Firewall | `conflictOfInterestFirewall` | Candidate-linked actors cannot dominate admissible evidence. |
| 9 | BPR-09 | Burden-of-Proof Router | `burdenOfProofRouter` | KEEP/CUT burdens come from explicit external policy; CUT cannot be weaker than KEEP. |
| 10 | RAC-10 | Reversible Archive Contract | `reversibleArchiveContract` | CUT means reversible non-destructive disposition with evidence retention. |
| 11 | AEC-11 | Appeal Eligibility Compiler | `appealEligibilityCompiler` | Material evidence delta/procedural defect can open appeal without restoring vote rights. |
| 12 | EDRG-12 | Evidence-Delta Reconsideration Gate | `evidenceDeltaReconsiderationGate` | Sham re-votes with no material delta are blocked. |
| 13 | PCM-13 | Precedent Consistency Monitor | `precedentConsistencyMonitor` | Divergence from comparable precedent requires an explicit distinguishing reason. |
| 14 | DIL-14 | Dissent Integrity Ledger | `dissentIntegrityLedger` | Opposing evidence remains preserved and attributable instead of being erased by the winner. |
| 15 | PDR-15 | Procedural Deadlock Resolver | `proceduralDeadlockResolver` | Symmetric proof deadlock returns DEFER, never an arbitrary tie-break. |
| 16 | ECG-16 | Explanation Completeness Gate | `explanationCompletenessGate` | Reason, counterargument, final justification and governing rule IDs are mandatory. |
| 17 | ECL-17 | Evidence Concentration Limiter | `evidenceConcentrationLimiter` | One evidence family cannot dominate impact beyond external policy. |
| 18 | ACF-18 | Assumption Contamination Firewall | `assumptionContaminationFirewall` | ASSUMPTION/UNKNOWN cannot be necessary to cross an affirmative proof burden. |
| 19 | TCT-19 | Temporal Causality Tribunal | `temporalCausalityTribunal` | Evidence/prior verdict ordinals must causally precede the transition they justify. |
| 20 | FIH-20 | Framing Invariance Harness | `framingInvarianceHarness` | Presentation/order changes cannot alter the semantic court result. |

These mechanisms are intentionally procedural. They answer: **“Was the recommendation reached by a robust court process?”**, not **“May this proposal become Canon?”**
