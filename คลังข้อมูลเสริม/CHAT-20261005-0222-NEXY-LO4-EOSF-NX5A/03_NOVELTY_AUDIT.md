## Novelty delta of final five systems
### ISC — Idempotency Scope Compiler
Current NEXY law requires idempotency keys. ISC asks a narrower, deeper question: **what semantic dimensions must the key bind so two different legal mutations cannot alias the same dedup identity?** It also distinguishes completed, in-flight and retryable prior executions.

### RAB — Retry Amplification Bounder
Current NEXY law says no automatic retry by default and retries only when safe. RAB computes the exact worst-case effect-attempt amplification of a bounded retry/fanout graph and refuses every multi-attempt node lacking explicit safe-retry authority.

### LFCG — Lease Fencing Commit Gate
This targets the stale-worker problem after lease reacquisition: possession of an old worker lease is insufficient; every commit must carry the current monotonically increasing fence.

### OES — Outbox Effect Seal
This models effect intent as `PREPARED -> COMMITTED -> EMITTED`, performs exact structural replay reconciliation, suppresses already-emitted effects, refuses cancelled identity reuse, and detects registry/outbox contradictions.

### CCC — Cancellation Closure Certifier
Current queue law says FREEZE/STOP cancels jobs. CCC computes the affected subtree and additionally requires every already-materialized external effect, regardless of job state, to have a declared compensation obligation.
