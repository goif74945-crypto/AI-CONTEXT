# Counterfactual Verification
Verify not only success but correct behavior when decisive premises change.
Tests: remove decisive evidence; mutate authority; introduce contradiction; stale a dependency; revoke capability.
Metamorphic properties: irrelevant evidence must not change verdict; duplicated evidence must not increase authority; reordering independent evidence must not change deterministic verdict; removing non-decisive evidence preserves verdict; changing decisive evidence changes verdict or forces re-verification.
Use: expose brittle judges, majority-vote errors, prompt-order effects and fake determinism.
