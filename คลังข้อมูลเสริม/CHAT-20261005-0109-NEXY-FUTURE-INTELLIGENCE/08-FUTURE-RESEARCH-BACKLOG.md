## Research acceptance template
Question; why it matters; authoritative inputs; experiment; falsification condition; evidence; result; limitations; adoption decision; review date.

## Priority principle
Favor work that prevents irreversible errors, false completion, fabricated truth, and silent requirement loss before convenience features.
