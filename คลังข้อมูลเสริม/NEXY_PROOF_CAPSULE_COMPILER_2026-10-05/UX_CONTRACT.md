# UX Contract — PROPOSAL ONLY

PASS must expose a concise result, capsule ID, required claims, selected evidence, any resolved dissent, target/version and relevant `as_of`. FREEZE must expose explicit state and stable reason codes and must not fabricate a substitute result.

The UI may hide graph noise by default but must allow drill-down to full lineage. It must not replace evidence with a confidence score, call something verified without the required evidence class, or hide dissent merely for visual cleanliness. The capsule reduces proof volume, not truth.
