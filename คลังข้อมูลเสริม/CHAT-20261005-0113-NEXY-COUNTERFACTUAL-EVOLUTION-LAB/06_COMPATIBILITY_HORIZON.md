# Compatibility Horizon
> AI-PROPOSED CONCEPT.
Record producer/consumer versions, schema/event version, min/max support, deprecation point, last-write-compatible point, last-read-compatible point, rollback horizon, and evidence-expiry conditions.
GREEN: declared bidirectional compatibility proven.
AMBER: one-way compatibility or incomplete proof.
RED: incompatible overlap.
UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence.
Any material code/config/schema change invalidates affected compatibility evidence until revalidated.
