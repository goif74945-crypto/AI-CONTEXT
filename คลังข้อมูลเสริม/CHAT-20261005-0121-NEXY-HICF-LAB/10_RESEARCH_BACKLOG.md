# 10 — AI-PROPOSED RESEARCH BACKLOG

These are ideas only, not requirements.

## R1 — Action-relative materiality engine
Classify whether an unknown matters to the *next specific action*, rather than assigning one global materiality value.

## R2 — Staleness-aware answer cache
A previous answer remains reusable only while its provenance dependencies remain valid.

## R3 — Clarification batching optimizer
Combine independent required questions into the smallest high-information interaction without hiding reason codes.

## R4 — Intent delta proofs
Produce a minimal machine-readable proof explaining why a resumed task is semantically equivalent to its prior objective.

## R5 — Consent-scoped personalization
Model explicit consent, expiry, supersession, and project boundaries for preference storage.

## R6 — Cross-model interaction consistency
Given the same HICF state, different AI workers should converge on the same control decision even if wording differs.

## R7 — Ambiguity value-of-information
Estimate whether asking a question materially changes the action set. Must remain subordinate to hard authority/safety gates.

## R8 — Human correction feedback without silent law evolution
Use corrections to propose candidate rules, but require explicit verification/adoption before promotion.

## R9 — HICF decision signing
Bind intent fingerprint, action proposal, decision, authority refs and checkpoint version to a tamper-evident record.

## R10 — UX truth surface
Render a compact explanation such as “Need target environment before write” while retaining full machine-readable reason evidence behind the UI.
