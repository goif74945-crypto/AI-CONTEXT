# NEXY Knowledge Foundry Manifest

Purpose: provide future NEXY.AI work with reusable engineering intelligence rather than speculative feature ideas.

## Design rules
1. Evidence beats confidence.
2. Unknown stays UNKNOWN.
3. Requirements are immutable unless the authoritative spec changes.
4. Every proposed change must expose blast radius, compatibility risk, rollback, and validation.
5. No production claim without executable or directly inspectable evidence.
6. Separate product truth, implementation truth, runtime truth, and research hypotheses.
7. Prefer compact high-signal artifacts over token volume.
