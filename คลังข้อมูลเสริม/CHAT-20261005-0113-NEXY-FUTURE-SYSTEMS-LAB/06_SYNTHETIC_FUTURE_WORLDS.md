# Synthetic Future Worlds
> AI-PROPOSED CONCEPT
Test architecture against futures that do not exist yet without pretending forecasts are facts.
## Generator dimensions
Model intelligence: lower/equal/higher/heterogeneous.
Inference price: 0.1x/1x/10x/100x.
Latency: local/regional/degraded/intermittent.
Regulation: permissive/provenance-heavy/action-restricted.
Network: stable/partitioned/offline-first.
Scale: 1x/100x/10,000x.
Data: clean/stale/adversarial/contradictory.
Providers: diverse/duopoly/single-provider failure.
Agents: single/swarm/hostile mixed tenancy.
## Output
Broken assumptions; preserved capabilities; unsafe behaviors; migration needs; evidence gaps; recommended experiments.
Synthetic worlds are scenarios, not predictions.
