# Reversible Architecture Ledger
> AI-PROPOSED CONCEPT
Record decision ID, date, authority, problem, alternatives, evidence, assumptions, choice, rejected options, rollback prerequisites, migration indicators, review trigger, consequences, and validation.
Classes: R0 config rollback; R1 adapter migration; R2 bounded data migration; R3 cross-service migration; R4 identity/schema breaking change; R5 effectively irreversible without product disruption.
Higher irreversibility should require stronger evidence and explicit acceptance criteria.
