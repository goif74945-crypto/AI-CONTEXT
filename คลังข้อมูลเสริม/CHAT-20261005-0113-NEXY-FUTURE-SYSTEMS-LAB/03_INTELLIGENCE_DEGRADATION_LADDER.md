# Intelligence Degradation Ladder
> AI-PROPOSED CONCEPT
Goal: remain truthful and useful when high-end AI capabilities fail, exceed budget, or lose authorization.
## Tiers
T0 full preferred capability.
T1 verified compatible alternate.
T2 narrower tasks + stricter confidence gates.
T3 deterministic assist: retrieval/rules/validation/cached verified artifacts.
T4 read-only survival; unsafe mutation disabled.
T5 safe refusal.
## Invariant
Degradation must reduce capability before it reduces truthfulness.
## Forbidden
Silent incompatible provider swap; pretending tools executed; presenting cache as current; UI success after backend guarantees vanish; hiding reduced verification depth.
## Test
Remove the strongest dependency of every critical capability and prove landing on a declared tier.
