# Proof-Carrying Delivery
A completion claim requires a proof bundle: objective snapshot, frozen requirement IDs, artifact list, verification actions, durable result references, negative tests, limitations, UNKNOWNs, regression assessment, provenance, and gate-derived status.

COMPLETE iff all critical RIDs are implemented, required evidence exists, no critical contradiction is unresolved, no known critical regression remains, and deliverables exist.

Evidence: E0 claim; E1 inspection; E2 deterministic check; E3 independent cross-check; E4 adversarial/negative validation; E5 repeated/production observation. Critical claims should prefer E2+ and E4 for forbidden behavior. "Looks correct" is not proof.
