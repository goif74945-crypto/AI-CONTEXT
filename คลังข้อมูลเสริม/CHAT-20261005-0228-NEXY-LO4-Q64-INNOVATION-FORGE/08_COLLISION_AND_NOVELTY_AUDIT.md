## Design distinction
This project does **not** attempt to replace those responsibilities. Its distinguishing unit is a shared Q64.64 quantitative decision microkernel plus twenty composable, narrow control transforms with one deterministic interface.

The Numeric Integrity Kernel is the closest numeric sibling, but it focuses on exact rational quantities/unit conversion/contract bounds. The Forge instead fixes an explicit Q64.64 representation and builds control-oriented quantitative modules (proof scheduling, consensus margin, degradation, authority gap, dependency confidence, etc.) on top.

## Collision found and repaired during design
The original twentieth concept was a safe experiment promotion gate. Inspection of Product Evidence Lab showed that this would materially overlap its evidence-to-experiment-decision role. C20 was therefore removed before persistence and replaced with `Dependency Confidence Firewall`, which propagates quantitative confidence through an explicit DAG and freezes weak critical dependencies.

That change caused one temporary stale test-block syntax regression, which was fixed and fully reverified. This collision-to-redesign lineage is intentional evidence that the non-duplication requirement was actually applied rather than merely asserted.

## Novelty claim boundary
This audit supports *repository-level distinctness from inspected siblings*, not global novelty or patent-style originality. No claim is made that no similar algorithm exists elsewhere in software research.
