# Future Research Roadmap

R1 Formal/model checking of canonical FSM reachability and forbidden transitions.
R2 Evidence-independence graph algorithms and circular-provenance detection.
R3 Deterministic distributed replay under concurrent agent scheduling.
R4 Byzantine and correlated swarm evaluation across shared model/prompt/retrieval/tool lineage.
R5 Semantic canonicalization fuzzing: Unicode, numbers, serialization order, duplicates, truncation, boundaries.
R6 Release-token lifecycle verification: issue, bind, consume, invalidate, freeze, recover, stale-token attacks.
R7 Durable evidence under crash faults at every write boundary.
R8 Authority-conflict resolver that flags descriptive docs contradicting executable/canonical specs.
R9 Regression corpus where every confirmed bug becomes minimized fixture + invariant + root cause + recurrence detector.
R10 Observability non-interference proving metrics/log/UI projections cannot authorize canonical state.

Prioritize by severity × authority impact × recurrence likelihood × detectability gap × reuse value. Treat qualitative inputs as ordinal rather than fake precision.

Research template: Question; Authority source; Hypothesis; Method; Fixtures; Oracle; Failure conditions; Evidence; Result; Limitations; Promotion decision.

Research becomes an engineering requirement only after mapping to authoritative scope and review. Interesting ideas do not silently become law.
