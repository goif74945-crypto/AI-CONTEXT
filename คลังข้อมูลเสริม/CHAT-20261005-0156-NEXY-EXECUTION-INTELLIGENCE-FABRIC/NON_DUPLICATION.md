## Deliberate separation
EIF does **not** implement another authority collision engine, provenance lattice, shadow replay engine, localization checker, generic capability admission gate, decision-stability scorer, or delta-impact analyzer.

Its primary novelty boundary is **execution economics + closure**:
- explicit goal completion predicates;
- minimum clarification set;
- minimum sufficient independent assurance set;
- precomputed rollback legality/order;
- contract-derived negative test generation.

## Potential conceptual adjacency
- Goal-State Compiler is adjacent to requirement ledgers, but it is runtime-evaluable and tamper-evident rather than documentation tracking.
- Unknown Closure Planner is adjacent to clarification policy, but it solves a deterministic exact set-cover problem over blockers/probes.
- Assurance Budget Planner is adjacent to SWARM/JUDGE verification, but optimizes validator selection under explicit cost/latency/independence constraints rather than adjudicating content.
- Reversibility Envelope is adjacent to preflight safety, but its contract is compensation completeness + dependency-safe rollback order + point-of-no-return identification.
- Counterexample Synthesizer is adjacent to testing tools, but generates negative vectors directly from field constraints and verifies every generated vector is actually rejected by the same validator.

No claim of global uniqueness is made. The claim is narrower: these implementations are materially distinct from the inspected adjacent AI-CONTEXT supplemental scopes.
