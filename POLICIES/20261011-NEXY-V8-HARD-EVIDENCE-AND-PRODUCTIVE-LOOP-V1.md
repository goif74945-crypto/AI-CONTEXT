## Non-negotiable engineering output

For every active task, choose one:
1. VERIFIED_FIX: reproduced old bad behavior (where practical), implemented complete scoped fix, observed GREEN+relevant regression, recorded exact commit and readback.
2. VERIFIED_COMPLIANT: runtime/current-source checks prove the atomic requirement was already satisfied; do not change source needlessly.
3. REPRODUCIBLE_FAILURE: real failure remains, with minimal reproducer, evidence hash, root-cause hypothesis clearly labeled, and next safe action.
4. INFRA_OR_AUTH_BLOCK: exact denial/error, independent equivalent tests where possible, blocked release gate and actionable handoff.

A prompt-only output, unsupported claim of test execution, repetitive generic report, guessed fix, or forced change-for-activity does not count as completed engineering.
