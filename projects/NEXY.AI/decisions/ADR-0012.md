# ADR-0012 — Robotics safety must be independent of AI reasoning

**Record type:** SOURCE_DERIVED_ADR  
**Authority:** RCL-L600, Final Architecture  
**Scope:** FUTURE_ARCHITECTURE

## CONTEXT
AI/model latency or incorrect reasoning can have physical consequences.

## OPTIONS
1. AI decides and executes directly
2. Use only software checks in same AI process
3. Separate Fast Brain/Safe Brain and independent Safety Kernel/MCU

## DECISION
Physical safety authority is independently capable of stopping actuation; AI is not sole safety mechanism.

## WHY
- Immediate hazards cannot wait for deep reasoning.
- AI/ROS failure must not disable emergency stop.
- Safety dominates intelligence.

## CONSEQUENCES
- Hardware/HIL/physical proof required.
- Reasoning and reflex paths have different timing contracts.

## SUPERSEDES
None explicitly recorded.

## ROLLBACK / CHANGE BOUNDARY
Would require a new safety architecture and domain-specific evidence.
