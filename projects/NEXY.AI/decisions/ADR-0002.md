# ADR-0002 — SWARM is labor; CORE/JUDGE own authority

**Record type:** SOURCE_DERIVED_ADR  
**Authority:** DOC-B, Trinity design  
**Scope:** CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE

## CONTEXT
External models and multi-agent work are probabilistic/untrusted and may agree incorrectly.

## OPTIONS
1. Give strongest model final authority
2. Majority vote
3. Use SWARM for proposals/attacks and retain final authority in CORE/LAW/JUDGE

## DECISION
SWARM/Lo3 may generate, debate and verify; final release authority remains outside worker models.

## WHY
- No single model becomes source of truth.
- Provider/model hot-swap does not change authority.
- Supports adversarial verification.

## CONSEQUENCES
- Every external output is untrusted candidate.
- Direct SWARM→VAULT authoritative persistence is forbidden in DOC-C.
- Consensus alone is insufficient without release gates.

## SUPERSEDES
None explicitly recorded.

## ROLLBACK / CHANGE BOUNDARY
Would require system-law authority rewrite.
