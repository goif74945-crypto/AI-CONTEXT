# NEXY.AI Negative Example Corpus

## Purpose
Counterexamples that teach AI to detect patterns that look productive or plausible but violate NEXY authority/evidence/integrity laws.

## Files
- `negative.jsonl`
- `validation-report.md`

## Main anti-pattern classes
- FAKE_SUCCESS
- STALE_EVIDENCE
- HIDDEN_FALLBACK
- INVALID_FSM
- AUTHORITY_INVERSION
- SCOPE_CREEP
- UI_MASKING_FAILURE
- UNVERIFIED_RELEASE
- NONDETERMINISTIC_MUTATION
- TEST_GAMING
- FAKE_MIGRATION_PROOF
- INCIDENT_TRACEABILITY
- MISSING_AUTHORITY
- GOVERNANCE_BYPASS

Many examples are grounded in actual historical NEXY failures. Historical recovery patterns remain revision-bound.

## Rule
Negative examples are prevention intelligence, not current findings unless current evidence proves the same defect still exists.
