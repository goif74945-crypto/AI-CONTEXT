### Legacy 215-entry registry
**STATUS: DEPRECATED_UNRELIABLE_DO_NOT_USE**

- `entity_type`: MODULE | ENGINE | SUBENGINE | LAW | PROTOCOL | FSM | CONTRACT | DATA_MODEL | INVARIANT | PLANE | UI_SURFACE | EVIDENCE | HARDWARE_PATH;
- `parent_id`;
- `authority`;
- `scope`;
- `status`;
- `source_range`;
- `dependencies`;
- `failure_behavior`.

Only after deduplication and ontology normalization should NEXY publish an atomic count.
